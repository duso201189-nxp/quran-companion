### English

```
Qur'an Companion is a free Qur'an reading and study app — no ads, no
data collection, and fully offline.

READ
• All 114 Surahs, 6,236 Ayahs, verified Uthmani text
• Three independently toggleable text layers: Arabic, Latin
  transliteration, translation
• Vietnamese (Rowwad Translation Center) and English (Saheeh
  International)
• Mushaf and list modes, adjustable font size, focus mode
• Remembers where you stopped; jump to any Surah:Ayah

LISTEN
• Five reciters: Alafasy, Abdul Basit, Minshawi, Husary, Sudais
• Speed control, repeat, play from any Ayah
• Mini player follows you when you leave the reading screen

COMMENTARY
• Commentary for every one of the 6,236 Ayahs
• Two works: Al-Muyassar (Arabic) and Ibn Kathir abridged (English)

NOTES & SEARCH
• Bookmarks, favourites, notes, highlights, collections
• Full-text search across Arabic and Latin script

PROGRESS
• Reading streaks, daily goal, Khatm progress
• Spaced repetition and quick quizzes

HONEST ABOUT SOURCES
Every translation, commentary and recitation is credited inside the
app — author, publisher, version, licence and source address. See
Profile → Data sources.

PRIVACY
No accounts. No ads. No analytics. Your notes and progress never leave
your device. The app only uses the network when you press play.

IN THIS BUILD
• Audio stops when the screen locks (background playback not yet built)
• No cloud sync — data does not transfer between devices
```

**Both "IN THIS BUILD" sections stay in the listing.** Store copy is
the first place a user forms an expectation; disclosing the two real
limitations there costs a few installs and prevents every one-star
review that would have said "audio keeps stopping".
