iOS bundle identifier `com.duso.qurancompanion`)

describes what the app does and does not do with your information.

## Summary

Qur'an Companion has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its
own. Everything you create in the app — bookmarks, notes, highlights,
reading progress, memorisation plans — is stored only on your own
device. The app sends no personal information anywhere. Its only
outbound network activity is fetching recitation audio files, and
opening a link in your browser if you tap one.

## Scope

This policy applies to the Qur'an Companion application on the
platforms it is distributed for (Android, iOS, and the Web/PWA build).
It does not apply to any other product, service, or website.

It also does not apply to third-party websites the app can send you to,
or to the servers that host recitation audio. Those are operated by
other people and governed by their own policies. See "Third parties"
below.

## Information the app collects

**Qur'an Companion collects no personal information, and transmits no
personal information to the developer or to anyone else.**

There is no user account system, no sign-up, no login, and no
authentication of any kind. The app never asks you for a name, email
address, phone number, date of birth, postal address, government
identifier, payment or financial information, health information,
biometric information, contacts, photos, or precise or coarse location.
No such field or collection point exists in the app, and the app has no
server to send it to.

The developer receives no reports, statistics, telemetry, or messages
from your copy of the app.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores data locally on your device, in two on-device SQLite
databases and in the operating system's standard app-preferences
storage. This data never leaves your device except as described under
"Network activity" below. It includes:

- **Content you create:** bookmarks and bookmark collections,
  highlights, personal notes, favourites, per-ayah reading status,
  study-session history, Khatm (completion) tracking, spaced-repetition
  card state, flashcard decks and cards, quiz results, Hifz
  (memorisation) plans, and review events.
- **App settings:** theme, language, selected reciter, daily-goal
  targets, and reading-display preferences.
- **Downloaded recitation audio:** audio files cached on your device
  after download, named only by surah and ayah number. These files
  contain no identifier for you.
- **Bundled reference content** (shipped with the app, not information
  about you): the Qur'an text, translations, transliteration, and
  reciter metadata, in a read-only database included in the app
  package.

**How this data is used:** solely to provide the app's features on your
device — to show you your notes and bookmarks, resume where you left
off, track your own reading and memorisation progress, apply your
settings, and play audio you have already downloaded. It is used for no
other purpose. It is not analysed, profiled, monetised, sold, or
shared.

The in-app statistics and progress screens are calculated on your
device from this local data. They are not telemetry and send nothing
off-device.

## Network activity

The app makes three kinds of outbound network request. There are no
others.

1. **Downloading recitation audio.** When you play or download a
   recitation, your device requests the corresponding audio file
   directly from **everyayah.com**. All recitation addresses included
   with the app use `https://`.
2. **Streaming recitation audio.** When audio is played before it has
   been cached, your device's own media player fetches the same
   `https://everyayah.com` file directly as it plays.
3. **Opening the Tanzil.net link.** The "Data sources" screen credits
   the Tanzil Project, as its licence requires, and shows a link to
   `https://tanzil.net`. If — and only if — you tap that link, the app
   asks your device to open that address in your browser or another
   app. The app itself sends nothing to tanzil.net.

In every case the request is made directly from your device to that
website. It is not routed through any server operated by the developer,
because none exists.

As with any request to any website, the connection itself necessarily
reveals ordinary technical information to the site being contacted —
including your device's IP address, and, for a link opened in your
browser, whatever your browser normally sends. **The app attaches no
account identifier, advertising identifier, device identifier, or other
personal information to these requests.** Which surah and ayah you are
listening to is inherently part of the audio file address you request.

The app declares the Android permissions `INTERNET` and
`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` (to make the audio requests above),
`FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`, and
`WAKE_LOCK` (to keep playback running with the screen off and show
media controls). It requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts,
or storage permission.

## Third parties

**Qur'an Companion shares no user data with any third party.**

The app contains no analytics service, no advertising network, no
crash-reporting service, and no third-party SDK that receives
information about you. There is no parent company, subsidiary, or
related entity, and no such entity has access to user data — there is
no collected user data for anyone to have access to.

Because the app transfers no user data to any third party for
processing or for that party's own use, no third party holds user data
on the app's behalf, and no third party is in a position to apply a
lower standard of protection to it than this policy states.

The two websites your device may contact — **everyayah.com** (audio)
and **tanzil.net** (only if you tap the credit link) — are independent
services operated by other parties. They receive only the connection
metadata inherent in the request, as described above. This policy does
not, and cannot, make representations on their behalf: their own
policies and practices govern what they do with the requests their
servers receive.

Other sources are credited in the app because their licences require
attribution for content bundled with it — the Tanzil Project (Arabic
Qur'an text and the Saheeh International English translation),
QuranEnc.com (Vietnamese translation by the Rowwad Translation Center),
Quran.com / QUL (transliteration), and the King Fahd Glorious Qur'an
Printing Complex (the UthmanicHafs font). These are content-attribution
relationships only. That content ships inside the app; those
organisations receive no data from the app and are not contacted by it.

## No advertising, analytics, or tracking

- No advertising is shown, and no advertising network is integrated.
- No analytics or usage-measurement service is integrated.
- No crash- or error-reporting service is integrated. The app's internal

- No Android Advertising ID, Apple IDFA, device fingerprinting, or

Separately, Google Play, Apple, and your device's operating system may
collect their own diagnostic or usage information under their own
policies, independently of this app and outside the developer's
control. That is governed by those companies' policies, not by this
one.

## Retention and deletion of your data

Because there is no account and no server, there is **no copy of your
data anywhere except on your own device**, and therefore nothing for the
developer to retain, disclose, or delete. The developer cannot access,
recover, or delete your data, and cannot identify you.

Your data is kept on your device for as long as you keep the app
installed. You are in full control of deleting it:

- **Delete everything:** uninstall the app, or clear the app's data
  through your device's system settings (on Android, Settings → Apps →
  Qur'an Companion → Storage → Clear data; on iOS, delete the app).
  This removes the local databases, your settings, and all cached audio.
- **Delete downloaded audio only:** use the in-app cache controls to
  clear cached recitations, either all of them or those of a single
  reciter, keeping your notes and progress.
- **Delete individual items:** bookmarks, notes, highlights and similar
  items can be deleted individually inside the app.

**Withdrawing consent.** Because the app collects nothing and sends no
personal information anywhere, there is no ongoing data processing to
withdraw consent from. To stop the app's only outbound activity, stop
playing or downloading audio, or uninstall the app. Deleting the app
deletes the data.

There is no "export my data" or "delete my account" feature, because
there is no account and no server-side data — your data is in your own
device's storage.

If you have a question or request about your information, email
**qurancompanionhq@gmail.com**.

## How your data is handled and protected

Your data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to the developer,
not stored on any server, and not shared. This is the app's primary
protection: the data is never collected in the first place.

Two limitations are stated plainly rather than glossed over:

- The app applies **no additional application-level encryption** to its
  local databases. It relies on the device-level encryption your
  operating system provides, and on your device's lock screen. Anyone
  with unlocked access to your device can read your notes and other
  app data.
- All recitation audio addresses shipped with the app use `https://`.
  The app does not control, and this policy does not warrant, how the
  independently operated everyayah.com servers respond to those
  requests.

This section describes how the app is built. It is not a guarantee of
security against every possible threat.
