What it does

Qur'an Companion is a reading and study application for the Qur'an. It works entirely offline: the complete text, translations and commentary are stored on your device. The only time it uses the network is when you press play on a recitation.

  • All 114 Surahs, 6,236 Ayahs, verified Uthmani text
  • Arabic, Latin transliteration and translation as three independently toggleable layers
  • Vietnamese and English translations
  • Commentary (tafsir) for every one of the 6,236 Ayahs
  • Five reciters, with speed control and repeat
  • Bookmarks, notes, highlights, collections and full-text search
  • Reading streaks, daily goal and Khatm progress

Privacy in one paragraph

There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. Your notes, bookmarks and reading progress are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. Playing a recitation fetches an audio file from a third-party server, which necessarily sees your IP address — that is the only data that ever leaves your device, and it is described in full in the privacy policy.

Sources and attribution

The Qur'anic text, translations, commentary and recitations in this application are the work of other people. Each is credited inside the app — author, publisher, version, licence and source address — on the Profile → Data sources screen, generated from the shipped data rather than written by hand, so it cannot fall out of date.

The same information, together with the licences of every bundled font and software library, is on the Third Party Notices page.
